# BIBLIOTHECA — The Soul Manifesto

## 1. Philosophical Foundations & Sovereign Software

Bibliotheca is not merely a component library; it is a declaration of engineering values and aesthetic discipline. It exists to provide the foundational substrate for offline-first, sovereign document creation tools where the user retains absolute ownership over their data, their words, and their presentation.

### The Five Inviolable Virtues

1. **User Sovereignty & Local First**
   - Software must function without external intermediaries.
   - Core creation, editing, compilation, and export operations happen locally in the user's browser runtime.
   - Zero mandatory telemetry, zero tracking beacons, zero cloud lock-in.

2. **Typographic Dignity & Editorial Restraint**
   - Design is not decoration; design is structure, hierarchy, and legibility.
   - Rooted in classical typography, European editorial heritage, and balanced proportions.
   - Rejection of visual noise, garish gradients, arbitrary decorations, and trend-chasing churn.

3. **The Discipline of Simplicity (Ponytail Engineering)**
   - Always favor the shortest working path that solves the genuine problem.
   - Rely on native web standards, semantic HTML, and modern CSS custom properties before introducing external dependencies.
   - Never write speculative abstractions or wrappers that add indirection without measurable value.
   - Question every addition with YAGNI (You Aren't Gonna Need It).

4. **Longevity & Structural Rigor**
   - Write code that remains functional, legible, and maintainable five or ten years from today.
   - Zero tolerance for silent type compromises (`any`), unhandled edge cases, or broken accessibility paths.
   - Deterministic tokens, predictable APIs, and full WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance.

5. **Sobriety and Technical Elegance**
   - Professional tools communicate with clarity, precision, and calm confidence.
   - Strict avoidance of emojis in code, commits, pull requests, and technical documentation.
   - Restrained micro-interactions that serve human intent rather than demanding human attention.

## 2. Aesthetic Architecture: The Classical & The Contemporary

Bibliotheca marries two distinct artistic traditions:
- **Classical Editorial Balance**: The timeless weight of serif typography, generous margins, disciplined grids, and calibrated contrast inspired by book printing and academic press.
- **Contemporary Minimal Utility**: The tactile precision of macOS utility applications, crisp stone neutrals, deliberate border radiuses, and focused monochromatic surfaces.

### The Neutral Continuum
The foundation is built upon organic stone surfaces:
- Light Mode: Warm, tactile off-whites (`#f7f7f5`, `#ffffff`, `#fbfaf9`) reminiscent of fine archival paper.
- Dark Mode: Deep obsidian and slate depths (`#0d1117`, `#161b22`, `#21262d`) engineered for sustained focus in dim environments without ocular fatigue.

### The 7 Signature Accents
Color is applied with intention, never as passive wallpaper:
- **Amber Gold**: Warm editorial bronze and craft.
- **Lateralis Teal**: Petroleum balance, clarity, and architectural precision.
- **Classic Royal Blue**: Authoritative academic and engineering focus.
- **Executive Navy**: Deep corporate structure and institutional weight.
- **Forest Emerald**: Natural vitality, growth, and verified accomplishment.
- **Burgundy Rose**: Creative confidence, literary depth, and leadership.
- **Obsidian Slate**: Monochromatic minimalism and terminal clarity.

## 3. The Ethical Developer Compact

Every engineer, agent, and system operating on Bibliotheca pledges to:
- Respect the machine: Keep bundles small, computation efficient, and memory leaks nonexistent.
- Respect the user: Never manipulate, obscure, or sacrifice readability for aesthetic indulgence.
- Respect the craft: Commit atomic units of work, write truthful tests, and preserve the architectural boundary between agnostic primitives and domain-specific logic.
